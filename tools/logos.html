Every tuner app draws a tuning fork. This one is not a tuner — it is a second presence in the room, listening while you play. So each mark below tries to draw two things in relation rather than one instrument. Judge them small: an app icon spends its life at 32 px.

Marks are drawn, not stored — this page is the source. Pick one and it becomes tools/icon.html, which renders the home-screen icons, the maskable variant and the share image in one pass.
