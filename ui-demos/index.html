Ten directions

Every one shows the same four surfaces — tuner, the take's pitch trace, timing figures, library — so you're judging design, not features. These are aimed at current app design rather than at a theme: the earlier five borrowed from the cello's world, and the instrument-panel one turned out to read better as a demo than as something to use every day. Try them on your phone too.

Dark

1 · Aurora

Soft gradient · glass

Deep space with violet and mint light bleeding in behind frosted cards. Premium and calm; the glow does the work, not the borders.

3 · Neon Slate

Dense · dashboard

Developer-tool energy: tight slate panels, hairline dividers, electric lime on cyan. The most information-dense of the ten.

7 · Studio

Warm dark · rounded

Dark but warm — aubergine-black, peach and sand, generous corners. The note name is set in a gradient; everything else stays quiet.

8 · Sonar

Deep sea · glow

Underwater blues with an aqua trace that glows like a sonar sweep. Monospaced figures keep the readings instrument-like.

10 · Pop

Vivid · playful

Near-black with saturated colour, one gradient hero card, a different accent per statistic. The most energetic option here.

Light

2 · Paper

Quiet · spacious

Warm white, soft shadows, one forest green. Nothing shouts; the numbers and the trace are the only colour on the page.

4 · Soft

Rounded · friendly

Lavender-tinted white, deep corner radii, violet and mint. Approachable without being childish — closest in spirit to what you had.

5 · Mono

Black & white · one flare

Heavy rules, condensed display type, zero radius. Orange appears only where something is off pitch, so colour always means one thing.

6 · Native

iOS · system

Grouped inset lists, system font, translucent tab bar, Apple's system colours. Looks like it shipped with the phone.

9 · Editorial

Serif · rules, not cards

A serif display face over clean sans, sections divided by hairlines rather than boxed into cards. Reads like a well-set page.
