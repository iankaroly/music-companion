practice partner

It listens while you practice and tells you what actually happened — which notes ran sharp or flat, where the tempo moved, and how cleanly you land after a leap.

What do you play?
  • Tuner a dial, drones and a spectrum — for tuning up.
  • Record play something, then read it back note by note.
  • Library every take you keep, grouped into folders by piece.
  • Score load the part you are working on and see your playing marked onto it.
  • Coach what your takes say about you over weeks, not minutes.
  • Metronome subdivisions, a tempo trainer and a practice timer.

Everything stays on this device. The microphone is only used while you are on the Tuner or recording.

        Metronome pitch

        Semitones from where the click normally sits — down to keep it under what you are playing, up to hear it over a room. It sounds once as you let go, unless the metronome is already running.

        In tune means

        Sets the green band on the tuner and what counts as in tune in every report.

        Microphone

        Off by default: the microphone is handed back the moment nothing is listening, so the orange light is on only while the tuner is running or a take is recording. Staying ready holds it open instead — slightly quicker to start, at the cost of that light staying lit the whole time.

        Screen

        On by default: the display stays on the whole time the app is open, so it doesn't dim in the middle of a passage. A take carries on recording either way, even if the screen locks.

        Appearance

        System follows your device's light/dark setting.

        Counting…

        The backup holds every take's notes, timing and landings — the part the coach reads — plus folders and passages. Audio is left out to keep the file small; share individual takes from the library instead.

        Privacy

        Your recordings never leave this device. They are stored by the browser, on the phone or computer you played them on. There is no account, no server and nothing to sign into — once the app has loaded it makes no network requests at all, so there is nowhere for a take to go.

        The microphone is open only while you are on the Tuner tab or recording, and is handed back when you leave. Nothing listens in the background.

        The only things that ever leave are the ones you send yourself: sharing a take, exporting a WAV, or saving a backup file. Because everything lives in this browser, clearing its data — or deleting the app — deletes your recordings too, so keep a backup of anything you would miss.

        Page pedal

        Bluetooth page turners pair as keyboards, so any of them works here with nothing to set up — pair it in iPad Settings, open a score, and press it. If yours sends something this does not know, teach it below.

        Built-in keys: arrows, page up and page down, space and return.

        Apple Pencil

        Draws with the pencil for four seconds and says what this device reported — pressure, tilt, whether the fast samples arrive, whether a resting palm is turned away, and whether anything on the page got highlighted.

        Sound

        Plays four clicks and says what this device did with them — whether the audio is awake, which route it is on, and where the levels are.

        Microphone

        Asks this device for the microphone and says exactly what came back — useful when the tuner or a recording will not start.
